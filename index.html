01 FEATURED
Data Center Team Research in Progress

The Geography of AI Power Constraints: Where Can Electricity Infrastructure Support the Next Wave of Data-Center Expansion?

A global, scenario-based investigation of where electricity generation, transmission, interconnection, and other power-system constraints could limit the expansion of AI data centers.

The study will compare regions across North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, Latin America, the Middle East, and Africa, with selected country-level case studies.

Research Question

Under what conditions does electricity infrastructure become a binding constraint on AI data-center expansion, and where globally is this constraint most likely to emerge?

Proposed Analytical Contribution

Research approach Scenario analysis · Forecasting · Uncertainty analysis

About

Engineering Systems. Quantitative Research. AI.

I am an electrical engineer and quantitative researcher with professional experience spanning electricity infrastructure, power-system operations, engineering research, data analysis, and machine learning.

My research interests sit at the intersection of artificial intelligence, energy systems, physical infrastructure, technological development, and quantitative analysis under uncertainty.

This portfolio explores questions about the systems required for AI to scale and the economic and societal consequences of that technological development.

Research Focus

Understanding the physical and economic systems that determine how quickly transformative technologies can develop, scale, and diffuse.
