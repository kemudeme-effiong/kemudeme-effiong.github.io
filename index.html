M.Tech dissertation • 2025/2026

Predicting Ampacity of Overhead Transmission Lines Using Supervised Regression Machine Learning Algorithms

Developed Dynamic Line Rating prediction models using approximately 50,000 rows of meteorological and transmission operational data. Implemented Linear Regression, Polynomial Regression, Random Forest, KNN and SVR with Python, Pandas, NumPy and Scikit-learn. IEEE Std 738 thermal modelling was used for physics-informed validation.

MBA capstone • 2024

Integrated Business Strategy for Transborder Classroom

Applied business and strategic thinking to an education-technology venture, complementing my engineering and technology background with international business training.

B.Eng final project • 2015

Hatchery Incubator Temperature Control & RFID Remote Monitoring

Designed a wireless temperature monitoring and control system using Arduino Uno, ATmega328, XBee modules and RFID, with real-time telemetry, sensor-based automation and database logging.
